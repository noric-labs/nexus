The terminal runs Brogue CE, a roguelike (100x34). Move with hjklyubn or the arrow keys,
x to auto-explore, > to descend stairs, i for inventory, s to search, Z to rest, e to eat,
q to quaff, r to read, t to throw, <Esc> to cancel. If a new-game menu shows, start a new
game. Explore, descend, pick up and try items, and survive. Start by calling screen().
