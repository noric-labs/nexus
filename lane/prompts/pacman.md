The terminal runs pacman4console, a real-time Pacman. You are C; ghosts are &; dots (.)
score points and the * power pellets let you eat ghosts for a while. Steer with <Up> <Down>
<Left> <Right>; Pacman keeps moving in the last direction, so send one or two direction
keys per call, chosen from the newest screen. Clear every dot to finish the level; stay
away from ghosts unless you just ate a power pellet. Start by calling screen().
