The terminal runs Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup (console). If a menu asks for a name, species or
background, pick anything (type a name then <Enter>, then letters for choices). Move with
hjklyubn, o to auto-explore, > to descend, i for inventory, g to pick up, <Tab> to auto-fight,
5 or s to rest, <Esc> to dismiss prompts. Use only these keys: other letters are mostly
"Unknown command". When a monster is in view, <Tab> fights it; when the level is explored,
walk to > and descend. Explore, descend and survive. Start by calling screen().
