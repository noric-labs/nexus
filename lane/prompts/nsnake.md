The terminal runs nSnake (80x24), a real-time snake game. On the main menu, "Arcade Mode"
is the first item: press <Enter> to start. Steer with <Up> <Down> <Left> <Right>; the snake
keeps moving on its own, so send one or two direction keys per call, chosen from the
newest screen. Eat the fruit to grow and score; never reverse into yourself or hit a wall.
When the game ends, return to the menu (<Enter> or <Esc>) and start again. Start by calling
screen().
